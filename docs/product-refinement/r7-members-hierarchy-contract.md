# R7 project membership and nested work contract

R7 implements R0 D09–D13 with additive, versioned operations. Existing root tasks and flat Office subitems retain IDs, evidence and finance identities. Office membership remains `project_office_members`; Members and Offices must read that same roster. Personal projects retain R3 memberships and a separate personal descendant table. Unresolved Office planning and governed collaboration work retain their existing flat workflows and approval rules; they do not gain versioned delegation rights. Account roles and canonical Offices never change through staffing.

## Membership and staffing

Current active, verified identities and joined operational Offices are required. Own appointed Heads select their Office's roster for this project only. Before enforcing selected-member eligibility, backfill currently staffed root/subitem people and existing project selections into their project's participating Office roster. Use the actual root lead in preference to its superseded planning recommendation when backfilling membership. Preserve stale/ineligible historical IDs, but deny their future execution. A diagnostic receipt records incompatible affiliation/Office cases rather than changing identity or silently granting cross-Office membership.

New assignments require explicit selected membership, including the lead Office. Heads retain oversight without being silently assigned. Admin and Observer gain no operational rights. Engagement labels are Permanent, Job Order, OJT, Consultant and Other; temporary labels require an exclusive UTC end instant or until-project-close term. Terms are membership metadata, not roles. New R7 operations evaluate these terms synchronously; external guest activation/dispatch remains R8 and integrated access reconciliation/removal remains R9.

Only the responsible appointed Head transfers an Office root lead; personal owner transfers a personal root lead. A current root lead can change contributors on that root via an exact contributor delta and appoint child leads. A node lead changes its contributors and descendants; only an authorized ancestor may replace that node's lead. No branch operation changes an ancestor/sibling, root lead, Office, project or finance fields. Replacing a lead invalidates descendant lead appointments; current ancestry is rechecked on every operation. Unfinished assigned work blocks contributor/roster removal until R9's impact-confirmed atomic removal is delivered.

## Tree and execution

- Root depth is 0; direct subitems are 1; maximum depth is 8. Same root/project, no cycles/self-parent, valid active ancestor chain. Creation, movement and ordering serialize on the root and compare the expected tree revision. Stable request UUIDs return committed receipts; changed retry payloads conflict.
- Sibling ordering is distinct from the legacy global position. Existing direct items retain their flat ordered/standalone semantics. Nested ordered work waits for earlier non-standalone siblings. Parent progress may start while descendants execute, but parent submission/approval and root completion require every descendant approved. Manual root progress is preserved; a separate leaf completion summary under the existing supported work statuses counts each leaf once.
- Reparent/order/mode/deletion affect untouched work only. Reparent checks both source and destination authority, entire subtree depth and deadline bounds. Child due dates cannot exceed any ancestor/root deadline; shrinking a date below a descendant requires editing that descendant first. No implicit ancestor extension.
- No new arbitrary dependency graph is introduced: retain root-task dependencies and sibling ordered/standalone prerequisites. Cross-branch execution dependencies are rejected rather than interpreted as root-relative positions. Deadlines and existing reminders still operate on canonical descendant rows; report/work adapters add ancestor context. No new reminder scheduler is introduced.
- An Office child submission retains existing evidence/cash guards. Reviewer is the nearest current eligible appointed ancestor lead, then current responsible Head when the lead is the submitter/ineligible. Self-review and stale/revoked reviewer authority are denied. Old decision authors remain unchanged. Personal descendants use independent personal reviews and no Office evidence/finance authority.
- Subtree deletion requires every node untouched and no evidence, progress history, allocations, budget caps or cash records. Completed/submitted history is preserved. Project completion/archive and root submission include all descendants, not only rendered rows.

## Integration and release

Versioned reads expose ancestry, depth, sibling order, lead, revision, effective permissions and selected eligible people. Existing flat consumers keep a complete descendant list, with explicit hierarchy labels/order; reports, My Work, activity and notifications retain canonical IDs. Templates/imports create direct subitems until their inputs explicitly carry reviewed hierarchy; exporting/applying a nested template must preserve or reject hierarchy explicitly, never flatten it silently. New protected tree data uses authorized reads/polling, not a new Realtime publication.

The R7 migration depends on Phase 6.5, R3 and R6 and is rehearsed on disposable PostgreSQL. It is not applied by implementation.
